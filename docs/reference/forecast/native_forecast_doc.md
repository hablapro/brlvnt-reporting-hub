# FOREX.com Native Advertising Forecast: US Hispanic and LATAM

Prepared for: FOREX.com / StoneX
Prepared by: Berelvant
Campaign window: 3 months (Month 1, Month 2, Month 3)
Budget: $10,000 per month per region, $30,000 per region over three months

---

## Read this first

This is a planning forecast built on benchmark assumptions and a directional read of the live Azerion programmatic data in the FOREX.com accounts. It is not a guarantee or a commitment to specific volumes. We present three scenarios so planning, finance, and operations can prepare for a range of outcomes.

Native advertising has no direct history in these two segments yet. We baseline it on the closest analog we run today: Azerion premium-publisher content placements (Reuters, CNN, CBS News, New York Times, Trading Economics). We then apply the native-format multipliers from the forecast brief. Treat the ranges below as the planning unit, not the point estimates.

We will recalibrate this model after 4 to 6 weeks of live native delivery and replace the benchmark-derived assumptions with measured native values.

The forecast stops at Registration (Step 1, Application Start). Submitted Applications and Funded Accounts sit deeper in the funnel and are not yet reliably tracked across these channels. See Data quality notes.

## How we built this

### Formulas (applied identically across every scenario)

- Clicks = Budget / native CPC
- Impressions = Clicks / native CTR
- Registrations = Clicks x native Click-to-Registration rate
- Cost per registration = Budget / Registrations

### Why Azerion is the baseline

Native ads run as in-feed and content-recommendation units on open-web publishers. The closest inventory we run today is Azerion, which serves content-style placements on premium publishers. We use the April 2026 Azerion numbers as the display baseline, since April was the first clean month after the March ramp-up.

| Region | Source | Baseline CPC | Baseline CTR | Baseline Click-to-Reg |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| US Hispanic (GCG) | Azerion, April 2026 | $3.83 | 0.16% | 5.0% |
| LATAM (GGMI) | Azerion, April 2026 | $4.29 | 0.105% | 5.0% |

Baseline derivation:
- US Hispanic CPC: $15,186 spend / 3,965 clicks. CTR: 3,965 clicks / 2,531,064 impressions.
- LATAM CPC: $10,215 spend / 2,380 clicks. CTR: 2,380 clicks / 2,269,903 impressions.
- US Hispanic Click-to-Registration: the April Azerion funnel disclosed roughly 10,000 content visits producing 492 Step 1 registrations, a 4.9% registration rate. We use 5.0%.
- LATAM Click-to-Registration: April Azerion recorded 19 full applications. Applying the US Hispanic registration-to-application rate of 15.4% (76 applications from 492 registrations) implies about 123 registrations, or 5.2% of 2,380 clicks. We use 5.0% to keep both regions on a single, conservative baseline that also matches the approved PMAX forecast.

### Native multipliers (from the forecast brief)

| Metric | Conservative | Expected | Aggressive |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| CTR (both regions) | 1.2x | 1.5x | 2.0x |
| CPC, US Hispanic | 1.3x | 1.2x | 1.0x |
| CPC, LATAM | 1.0x | 0.9x | 0.75x |
| Click-to-Registration (both regions) | 1.0x | 1.15x | 1.3x |

Native typically lifts CTR over standard display. US Hispanic CPCs hold near or slightly above display because US premium inventory prices high. LATAM CPCs come in lower because Spanish-language inventory in Mexico clears cheaper. Registration rates get a modest lift for well-localized, content-led traffic, capped at 30%.

## Data quality notes

- Display attribution is view-through only. Both Azerion and Quantcast recorded only view-through conversions through April, with the click-through pixel still in validation with the Central Team. We do not derive the click-to-registration rate from display click conversions. We use the disclosed Azerion content funnel instead and document it as an assumption.
- Funded accounts are not tracked in any connected platform. The deepest reliably tracked step is Registration (Step 1, Application Start), which is why the forecast terminates there. A Registration-to-Funded factor validated from StoneX CRM would be required to extend this model to revenue.
- The LATAM registration rate is derived, not directly disclosed. Azerion reported full applications for LATAM but not the Step 1 count. We back into it using the US Hispanic funnel shape.
- Azerion frequency and spend reconciliation carry minor flags from the monthly reviews. They do not change the CPC and CTR baselines used here.
- This is the first native flight for both segments. First-flight forecasts carry wider variance than forecasts for established channels.
