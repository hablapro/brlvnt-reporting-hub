# FOREX.com September Budget Evidence Base

**Markets:** GCG, FOREX.com US Spanish; GGMI, FOREX.com Mexico
**Prepared:** August 28, 2026
**Purpose:** Establish the source of truth required to revise the September Recommended Spend and Minimum Spend proposals.
**Decision status:** Evidence assessment only. This document does not assign final Scale, Continue, Test, Pause, or budget decisions.

## Reading rules

- Client budget trackers are the source of record for planned and reconciled spend when available.
- Platform exports are authoritative for platform delivery. Partner files are authoritative for partner-reported delivery and outcomes, but partner-reported applications are not assumed to be deduplicated or directly comparable with search submissions.
- Search, Azerion, and Quantcast conversion signals must be read independently. They cannot be summed into a cross-channel application total or blended CPA.
- Submitted applications are the media acquisition outcome currently available at channel level. Approval, funding, first trade, revenue, and profitability remain downstream business outcomes unless source-level matching is available.
- Estimates and projections are labeled. Missing values are shown as unavailable.
- The August drafts under each market folder are working history. The August 27 files in `_final-delivered/2026-09/` are the current pre-feedback proposal baseline.

Mateus's framework requires continued investment to be supported by current results, or by a defined hypothesis, optimization action, success threshold, and viable test window. It also excludes new September initiatives and token test spend. Source: `/Users/rpro/Downloads/Plan.md`, lines 1-27, 110-147, and 199-220.

## Evidence-stage conclusion

The available record is strong enough to classify the active acquisition and display lines provisionally. It is not strong enough to finalize revised September budgets yet without first translating each retained or tested line into a hypothesis, optimization action, KPI, threshold, valid evaluation period, and meet-or-miss decision rule.

The most decision-relevant evidence is:

1. Search remains the clearest directly attributable acquisition layer, but both accounts require conversion-action filtering and both are in operating states that weaken simple run-rate extrapolation.
2. Azerion produces partner-reported applications in both markets. GCG shows a favorable trend; GGMI shows continued volume but weakening marginal efficiency. Full August files and downstream matching would materially strengthen both conclusions.
3. Quantcast can be evaluated for reach, CPM, viewability, traffic quality, placement quality, and its own platform-result signal. It cannot be ranked against search using submitted-application CPA, and the current lowest-cost-traffic wording is not supportable as written.
4. Native lacks the measurement and test design required to justify token September spend.
5. The proposed new or returning social, video, and partnership lines do not currently have enough September-ready evidence to support a performance classification. GCG Meta is distinct from CTV and TikTok because it has delivery history, but the history does not provide a validated acquisition result.
6. Source-level downstream data, routing validation, geo compliance, creative readiness, and approval timing are shared operating conditions. They affect whether a media test is fair and decision-useful, but do not replace the need for accountable media optimization.
