# GGMI (Mexico) — Q4 FY26 Budget Recommendation & FY2027 Proposal

DRAFT for Renzo review, 2026-07-07. Client-facing once approved. Companion workbook: `GGMI-Q4-FY2027-budget-recommendation.xlsx` (Sheets-ready). Basis: client tracker (fee-inclusive), fiscal year Oct–Sep.

## Where the budget stands

FY26 budget $1,044,000. Spent through June: $320,575 (30.7%). Remaining for Q4 (Jul–Sep): $723,425. The underspend traces to launch timing on the client side (compliance changes, operational readiness), not to media capacity or performance. That context matters for what follows: the right Q4 plan grows from what the data proved, rather than racing to deploy the full remainder in 90 days.

September becomes the largest month in account history (+58% over June's record), and every step-up is earned by a verifiable gate rather than scheduled on hope. It also opens the first two of the plan's untapped channel lines: Native (per the existing forecast brief) and Apple Search Ads — brand-defense and app installs that pair naturally with the new app-friendly KYC onboarding. Strategic Partnerships/Affiliates deliberately carries no Q4 spend: as a regulated product, the affiliate/IB model needs compliance scoping with the client's legal team first, which we do in Q4 so the channel can launch in FY27 Q1. Projected outcome on defined conversions: roughly **379 submitted applications vs ~162 in the comparable Q3 months** — about 2.3x the applications on +69% spend, because dollars move from out-of-market and low-quality delivery into gated Mexico delivery. Meta and Quantcast are projected at zero applications on purpose; anything they add after validation is upside, not promise.

**Why not deploy the full $723,425.** June was the proof month: at $120K, three of four channels showed quality strain (site-visit capture on paid social, viewability on programmatic reach, out-of-market search delivery). Mexico is the entire addressable market; pushing 2x June's spend into the same inventory buys worse placements at higher prices and puts the year's efficiency story at risk. Full-deployment and conservative-hold scenarios are in the workbook for comparison.

**The remaining ~$303K is not a media problem to solve — it's roadmap fuel.** Spending it on media pressure would repurchase June's quality problems at scale. The strategic uses of that gap are the things that make every future media dollar work harder:

- **Conversion infrastructure**: onboarding/CRO work around the new KYC flow, WhatsApp launch readiness (flows, templates, re-engagement journeys), and landing-page work for the start-to-submit leak.
- **Organic recovery**: the SEO/content program — the funnel's largest traffic source is the one that declined, and no media budget substitutes for it.
- **Brand foundation for the awareness push**: creative system, positioning, and localized assets, so FY27's awareness dollars have something distinctive to amplify.
- **Measurement**: the analytics fixes and cost/revenue feed repairs already scoped, so FY27 optimizes on funded accounts, not clicks.

Whether those investments sit inside this budget line or elsewhere in the client's P&L is their call — the recommendation is simply that the gap gets filled by the roadmap, not by media bought to hit a number.

**One dependency to name plainly:** the application funnel's approve-to-fund rate halved during H1 — and the client's own roadmap addresses it. Lowering the $100 minimum deposit (high for LATAM) and adding stablecoin funding attack that exact stage; the new Persona KYC flow should lift application completion; WhatsApp arrives in January. Q4's application volume converts to funded accounts as those land, so the media ramp and the onboarding roadmap should be read as one plan, not two.

## FY2027 proposal: $1.2M base + $300K performance-gated flex

FY27 carries two objectives, reported separately so neither corrupts the other's numbers, and funds every channel line in the media plan for the first time:

1. **Acquisition** on the proven converters — Search Mexico ($370K) and Azerion ($290K), 55% of base, judged on CPA — plus two high-intent additions: Apple Search Ads ($30K; brand defense and app installs feeding the new app-based KYC flow) and Affiliates/Strategic Partnerships ($60K; the most LATAM-native growth channel, launched in Q1 after the compliance scoping done in Q4).
2. **Awareness** — brand visibility in Mexico is effectively zero today, and applications can't scale past a brand nobody searches for. Quantcast (floored, $150K), Native ($90K), and TikTok ($40K) form the awareness stack alongside the client-led DOOH line, judged on reach, frequency, and branded-search growth (we already baseline brand queries in Search Console) — never on CPA.

Meta ($170K) bridges both: rebuilt on validated inventory, and from January it becomes the natural home for **click-to-WhatsApp campaigns** — with WhatsApp's penetration in Mexico, pairing its launch with paid support and WhatsApp re-engagement of stalled applications is the single most LATAM-native tactic available in FY27.

The $300K flex releases in quarterly tranches tied to milestones: Q2 ($75K) deploys into the WhatsApp launch if Q1 held its CPA and quality gates; Q3 ($100K) scales acquisition once the deposit reduction, stablecoin rails, and Persona KYC are live and approve-to-fund is recovering; Q4 ($125K) follows the Native pilot or remaining Search headroom. The budget grows by earning it, quarter by quarter, with quarterly go/no-go reviews replacing annual set-and-forget.

Riding with the media number, two structural recommendations:

1. **A dedicated SEO/content line, separate from media.** The majority of the client's application volume arrives through organic and direct demand, and organic search declined roughly 70% across H1 for content-related reasons we've verified in Search Console (aging analysis content, mid-funnel pages losing rank while brand rankings improved). Media cannot buy that volume back at acceptable cost; content investment can. This is the single highest-leverage line adjacent to the media budget.
2. **Measurement prerequisites**, carried from the July action list: the search-platform link into analytics, validated conversion events on paid social, and a standing monthly reconciliation of platform clicks against measured site sessions. With those in place, FY27 reporting can commit to cost per funded account rather than stopping at submitted applications.

## Conservative forecast: submitted applications

We commit to the floor, not the midpoint. This forecast counts only the two proven converters, degrades their CPAs 20% (Search $620, Azerion $1,000), assumes every scale-up gate slips a month, and takes zero credit for Meta, the awareness stack, the pilots, the flex tranches, or any onboarding improvement.

| Period | Conservative floor | Base case | What the floor assumes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q4 FY26 | **≈253 submitted apps** (~84/mo) | ≈379 | Effective converter spend $200K of $240K planned; all other channels count zero |
| FY2027 (base budget) | **≈812 submitted apps** (~68/mo) | higher with flex + validated channels | 85% deployment on converters only; $540K of awareness/test spend counts zero |

For reference, the same two channels produced ~162 submitted apps in May–June combined. The floor is the commitment; everything the plan's gates unlock — validated Meta, the WhatsApp launch, funnel fixes, pilots — lands on top of it. Funded-account outcomes are deliberately excluded from the media commitment: at current funnel rates ~25% of submits approve and ~10% of approvals fund (client-owned stages the deposit and payment-rail roadmap is built to fix).

## What we need from the client

**Data and tracker items:**
- Confirmation of the funnel file's cost-feed and revenue-column formulas (their tracker's internal cost basis doesn't match delivered media in any month).
- A funnel-by-country split, to separate media quality effects from market-mix effects.
- The DOOH line's status ($26,454 in June sits in the tracker but outside its totals) — whose line is it, and does it continue?

**Roadmap discovery — a working session, not an email.** We know four roadmap items (deposit reduction, stablecoin funding, Persona KYC, WhatsApp in January) and each one changed this plan. There are almost certainly others we don't know about, so before FY27 locks, we need the full picture:

1. The complete product and compliance roadmap for the next 12 months, with dates — what else is changing that we haven't heard about?
2. Planned promotions or incentives (deposit match, reduced spreads, referral rewards) that media should be timed to, rather than discovered after launch.
3. The app roadmap — is the strategy app-first? This decides how hard Apple Search Ads and app-install formats should work.
4. Any market expansion beyond Mexico under the license — it changes the addressable ceiling every capacity assumption in this plan rests on.
5. Brand assets and positioning available for the awareness push — or is brand development itself a gap to fund from the remainder?
6. Operations capacity: if applications double, can onboarding, verification, and support absorb them at today's staffing?
7. Cohort revenue/LTV economics (ties to the cost-feed question) — the number that turns our CPA targets from convention into math.
8. Payment-rail roadmap beyond stablecoin (SPEI, OXXO, local cards) — funding friction is the funnel's weakest stage; we should know everything planned for it.

The principle for both quarters: **we spend into verified capacity and real milestones, not into a budget line.** Every dollar that quality can't yet absorb goes to whatever the roadmap says removes the next constraint.
