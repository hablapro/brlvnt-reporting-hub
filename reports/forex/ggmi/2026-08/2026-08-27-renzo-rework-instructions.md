# GGMI Spend Reduction Rework Instructions

## Role
You are revising the GGMI Mexico spend-reduction recommendation for FOREX.com. Your job is to preserve the core strategic thesis, fix the financial modeling, remove weak or overstated claims, and produce a client-ready recommendation that is analytically defensible and easy for leadership to act on.

## Objective
Rework the current document so that it answers one decision clearly:

**How much should FOREX.com temporarily reduce GGMI Mexico media spend now, while preserving the ability to scale back up if market activity improves in September/Q4?**

The final recommendation must distinguish between:
- a **Controlled Pullback**; and
- a **Deep Pullback**.

Do not frame the reduction as permanent. Do not make stablecoin launch the sole trigger for restoring spend.

---

## Source of Truth
Use the existing GGMI spend-reduction document as the working draft, but correct it using the following verified operating facts:

- DOOH is already cancelled.
- Native, Azerion, and Quantcast are governed by IOs.
- Those IOs have a 24-hour cancellation window.
- There are no make-goods.
- Therefore these digital commitments are operationally flexible and can be reduced or cancelled quickly.
- July should be used as a **performance reference**, not automatically as the financial baseline for the scenarios.
- The scenarios should be modeled against the **current approved monthly budget or current August expected run rate**, whichever best reflects what FOREX.com is actually spending now.
- Bing is currently scaling above its July level, so using July Bing spend and labeling it “hold at pace” is incorrect.

If the current approved monthly budget or August projected month-end spend by channel is not available in the source material, flag that as the only required data gap and do not invent numbers.

---

## Required Strategic Position

Preserve this core recommendation:

> Reduce inefficient spend now, but treat the reduction as temporary and reassess in September using complete conversion data, actual FOREX.com funnel performance, and relevant market-activity indicators.

The final document should make these points:

1. Current weak funding does not automatically prove that media is the primary problem.
2. July/August may reflect a softer market environment and should not automatically become the Q4 planning baseline.
3. July conversion measurement was incomplete and must be treated cautiously.
4. Stablecoin may be a catalyst, but there is not yet evidence that it will materially improve funded-account conversion.
5. Market indicators such as CME FX CVOL and FX volumes are useful context, but they are **not automatic budget triggers**.
6. First-party business outcomes remain the primary decision criteria:
   - applications,
   - approvals,
   - funded accounts,
   - cost per funded account where available.

## Financial Model Corrections

### 1. Rebuild the baseline
Do not calculate reductions against July spend unless July is explicitly confirmed as the current approved budget.

Use, in order of preference:
1. current approved monthly budget;
2. projected August month-end run rate;
3. only if neither exists, clearly state that scenario percentages are provisional.

### 2. Protect acquisition lines based on current performance
Do not automatically hold Bing at its July spend level.

If Bing is pacing materially above July and is still the most efficient acquisition line, the modeled scenario should protect its **current productive run rate**, not reset it backward.

Evaluate Azerion similarly using the latest available performance and current run rate.

### 3. Reframe the two scenarios
Avoid calling a 55% reduction “Conservative.”

Use:

#### Controlled Pullback
A meaningful but measured reduction that:
- protects the strongest acquisition activity;
- keeps only the awareness/reach activity that has a defensible near-term role;
- preserves flexibility to scale back up quickly.

Target range, if supported by the actual current baseline: approximately **35–45% reduction**.

#### Deep Pullback
A larger cost-preservation move that:
- keeps only the most defensible acquisition activity;
- removes most or all awareness/reach spend;
- accepts the risk of restarting upper-funnel activity later.

Target range, if supported by the actual current baseline: approximately **55–65% reduction**.

Do not force the percentages into these ranges if the underlying channel economics point elsewhere. The numbers must follow the evidence.

## Channel Treatment

### Bing
- Treat as a protected acquisition line if current efficiency remains strongest.
- Use current run rate, not July spend, if it has scaled materially since July.
- Do not say “hold at pace” unless the proposed spend actually reflects current pace.

### Azerion
- Treat as an acquisition line because it reports submitted applications.
- Evaluate whether full current weight remains justified based on latest CPA and volume.
- Do not protect it automatically if more recent performance materially deteriorated.

### Quantcast
This is the main strategic decision between the two scenarios.

Do not justify keeping it merely because it provides “awareness.”

If recommending a reduced Quantcast line, explain the specific economic or operational value of keeping it live, such as:
- qualified traffic quality;
- retargetable audience accumulation;
- modeled audience continuity;
- assisted conversion evidence;
- incremental reach;
- restart/relearning cost or speed.

If those benefits cannot be demonstrated from available data, say so and weaken the case for keeping $19K+ per month active.

### Native
- Can be reduced or cancelled with 24-hour notice.
- No make-good obligation.
- If it has no attributable application outcome or stronger strategic role than Quantcast, it is a logical cut candidate.

### DOOH
- Remove it from the decision model as a future cancellation decision because it is already cancelled.
- It can remain in historical July tables for context, but clearly label it **already cancelled**.
- Do not count cancelling DOOH as a new saving generated by either scenario.

### Meta
- If already paused, treat it the same way.
- Do not present an already-paused line as a new scenario saving.

## Measurement Language

The current draft says that 15 conversion tags were “firing dead” and therefore July application totals are a floor.

Keep the measurement caveat only to the extent it is verified.

Preferred wording:

> **Measurement caveat:** July conversion reporting was incomplete because multiple application-related conversion activities were not recording correctly. July application totals should therefore be treated cautiously until tracking is fully restored and reconciled.

Do not claim that every broken tag represents a lost application unless that has been proven.

Elevate this caveat so leadership sees it before interpreting July efficiency.

---

## Funnel Language

Remove language that sounds like the agency is assigning blame to the client.

Do not use:

> “Approve-to-fund is a step you own, not one media controls.”

Replace with:

> “Funding performance reflects both acquisition quality and downstream approval and funding behavior, so media performance should be evaluated alongside the full application-to-funded-account funnel.”

The point is to separate funnel stages analytically, not defensively.

---

## Market and Seasonality Language

Avoid categorical claims such as:
- “August is a slow window for trading activity.”
- “Q4 is the strongest demand window.”
- “September will reactivate the market.”
- “When CVOL turns, scale spend back up.”

Use more defensible language:

> “Current market conditions appear relatively subdued, while volatility seasonality and forward-looking market indicators suggest conditions could become more active through September and Q4.”

Treat VIX/CVOL/volume as **context**, not forecasts or automatic budget triggers.

The decision hierarchy should be:

1. FOREX.com applications and funded-account performance;
2. complete/reconciled conversion measurement;
3. market activity and volatility context;
4. stablecoin/product timing.

---

## Stablecoin Position

Shorten the current stablecoin discussion.

Use one concise paragraph:

> “The stablecoin launch may improve accessibility and broaden acquisition opportunities, but there is not yet evidence that it will materially change funded-account conversion. It should therefore be treated as one potential catalyst, not the sole condition for restoring media investment.”

Do not turn the document into a debate about stablecoin adoption or product strategy.
