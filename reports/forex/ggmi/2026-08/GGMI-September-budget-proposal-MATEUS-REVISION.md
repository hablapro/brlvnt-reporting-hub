## **Azerion launch condition**

The Azerion allocation becomes active once the partner confirms Mexico delivery controls and a minimum **95% Mexico delivery standard**. Until that confirmation is received, the allocation remains unspent and is not moved to another line automatically.

The confirmation must arrive by September 3 to preserve the 28-day evaluation window. If it does not, neither the $90,000 Recommended Plan nor the $60,000 Minimum Plan remains authorized as designed. Bing and Quantcast may continue temporarily at the selected weights while the allocation returns for a revised decision. Azerion remains at $0 and is reconsidered in October.

# **4. Recommended Plan: $90,000**

## **Bing / SA360: up to $25,000**

Begin September at the $20,000 base level. Concentrate spend in verified platform-intent campaigns and apply query, negative, bid, destination, and pacing controls.

Release the final $5,000 after at least 15 Live Confirmation submissions at CPA of $400 or less, provided at least 95% of delivery remains in Mexico. Treat $25,000 as a cap rather than a forced-spend target.

## **Azerion display: $35,000**

Activate after the Mexico delivery condition is met. Prioritize Conversion Instruments, Commodities, and Experience inventory. Pause Platform and Global Market combinations that carried the weakest July application economics.

The test must produce a material improvement from July and partial August. Maintaining the same marginal CPA would not count as success.

## **Quantcast display: $30,000**

Use the productive main Q+ campaign and keep NativeOnly at $0. Apply the existing 66-site exclusion list, remove weak inventory, and review Mexico delivery plus site and app quality twice each week.

Quantcast will be judged on reach, CPM, viewability, geography, and placement quality. Platform results remain directional and will not be presented as submitted applications.
