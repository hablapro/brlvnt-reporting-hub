# GGMI Mexico: Temporary Media Pullback with a September Reassessment

**Entity:** GGMI, GAIN Global Markets Inc. (Mexico, forex.com/es)
**Prepared for:** FOREX.com
**Prepared by:** brlvnt
**Date:** August 27, 2026
**Baseline:** Current August 2026 run rate by channel
**Status:** DRAFT for FOREX.com review. Not for distribution.

---

## 1. Executive recommendation

brlvnt recommends a **Controlled Pullback**: a measured reduction of about 41% against the current August run rate, protecting the two lines that produce submitted applications, holding a reduced and monitored Quantcast line, and removing the reach spend that carries no near-term role. This holds monthly working media at about $66,421, down from the current active run rate of about $113,306.

The reduction is warranted now because funding reads soft and current market conditions appear relatively subdued, so lower-return spend can be paused without giving up the acquisition engine. It is a temporary reduction, and it is not gated on the stablecoin launch. The Controlled Pullback preserves the ability to scale back up quickly because it keeps a live, modeled audience rather than starting cold later.

The program is reassessed in September against first-party business outcomes and market context. That reassessment, not the calendar, decides when spend is restored.

---

## 2. What the current data does and does not tell us

**Funding reads soft, but that does not by itself identify media as the primary problem.** Weak funding can originate in acquisition quality or in downstream approval and funding behavior. The evidence to date establishes the outcome, not the cause, so the pullback is sized to reduce lower-return spend while a fuller diagnosis is completed.

**July is a performance reference, not the financial baseline.** July figures are used here to read line-level efficiency; the scenarios are modeled against the current August run rate, not July spend.

**Market context is subdued but not fixed.** Current market conditions appear relatively subdued, while volatility seasonality and forward-looking market indicators suggest conditions could become more active through September and Q4. These indicators are context, not forecasts, and not automatic budget triggers.

**Funnel stages should be read together, not assigned in advance.** Funding performance reflects both acquisition quality and downstream approval and funding behavior, so media performance should be evaluated alongside the full application-to-funded-account funnel. The point is to separate the funnel analytically, not to assign the outcome to any one stage before the evidence supports it.

On the stablecoin launch: the stablecoin launch may improve accessibility and broaden acquisition opportunities, but there is not yet evidence that it will materially change funded-account conversion. It should therefore be treated as one potential catalyst, not the sole condition for restoring media investment.

## 5. Scenario rationale

**Controlled Pullback.** Protects both application lines at current pace, including the rebuilt Bing account, which delivered the lowest cost per submitted application in the program in July and is scaling in August. Retires the superseded Quantcast campaign and holds the remaining productive Quantcast line at about half its own run rate, so a modeled, retargetable audience stays live and can be scaled quickly when demand turns, rather than being rebuilt from zero. Removes Native, which reports no attributed applications. The tradeoff is about $10,379 a month to keep a live, addressable audience and a faster restart.

**Deep Pullback.** Keeps only the two application lines and removes all reach spend, including Quantcast. It preserves the most cash while retaining the acquisition engine, and it accepts the cost and time of restarting upper-funnel activity later from cold. It is the stronger choice if cash preservation becomes the sole priority or the reassessment horizon extends well out, at which point the retargetable value of the Quantcast line no longer earns its monthly cost.

The Quantcast line is where the two scenarios genuinely differ, so its economics deserve a plain read. The retained Quantcast line carries a retargetable, modeled audience, and its productive campaigns began returning result signals in August where the native segment had shown none in July. Against that, the current Quantcast results are recorded as view-through with click attribution not yet confirmed, and the retargeting segment is a thin and expensive share of delivery. The Controlled Pullback therefore holds Quantcast as a monitored, low-cost option rather than a proven performer. If the September read does not show the productive Quantcast line improving its cost per result and attribution quality, the correct move is the Deep Pullback and a full cut.

---

## 6. Recommendation

**Take the Controlled Pullback as a temporary reduction with a September reassessment.** It answers the decision on the table: it reduces lower-return spend now, at about 41% against the current run rate, while protecting the acquisition engine and keeping the ability to scale back up quickly. A fully dark upper funnel would mean cold-starting the largest audience platform at the point demand is most likely to lift, and the modest cost of a reduced Quantcast line buys a live audience against that risk while its economics are watched. The Deep Pullback becomes the better call if cash preservation becomes the single priority, or if the September read does not improve the Quantcast line's cost per result and attribution quality.

The reduction is temporary by design and is not gated on the stablecoin launch. It runs until the September reassessment reads real demand and complete conversion data. What this avoids is a deep, open-ended cut sized to a subdued summer that leaves the program underweight going into a potentially more active quarter.

---

## 7. September reassessment

The program is reassessed in September against first-party business outcomes first, then market context, in that order:

1. **FOREX.com applications, approvals, and funded accounts**, the primary decision criteria.
2. **Cost per funded account where available**, read with care where denominators are small.
3. **The productive Quantcast line's cost per result and attribution quality**, which decides whether the reduced Quantcast spend is retained or cut.
4. **CME FX CVOL for the MXN contract and relevant FX volumes**, as market context only, not automatic budget triggers.
5. **Stablecoin launch status**, as a secondary input, not the condition that releases spend.

If those outcomes show demand turning, that is the signal to scale back up. First-party business outcomes outrank market context, which outranks stablecoin timing.
