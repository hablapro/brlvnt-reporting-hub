# **3. Recommended Plan: up to $110,000**

## **Google Search: $30,000**

Hold Search at its planned level. July impression-share loss came more from rank than budget, so the account should improve query quality, campaign weights, and ad relevance before receiving more money.

The strongest current campaign pool, Brand, TradingView, and MT5, receives priority. Platform and Forex remain active with tighter controls. Trust and Authority stay capped until their application CPA improves.

## **Google PMax: $10,000**

Run a capped September correction test after Step-5 becomes the decision goal. Report click-attributed and view-inclusive results separately. The campaign must meet the Sep 15 viability gate before completing the full allocation.

If the goal correction cannot launch by September 3, hold the allocation. Do not transfer it to another line without a fresh allocation decision.

## **Azerion display: $35,000**

Return Azerion to the planned September level and concentrate delivery in the stronger Professional Tools, Trust, and broker-oriented combinations. Cap weaker ad sets and review the mix each week.

The current efficiency signal supports investment, but September still needs to show that the result holds at greater scale.

## **Quantcast display: $30,000**

Use the productive Q+ structure and keep NativeOnly at $0. Apply the existing exclusion list, remove weak inventory, and review site and app delivery twice each week.

Quantcast will be judged as a reach and inventory-quality line. Platform results remain directional and will not be presented as submitted applications.

## **Conditional reserve: $5,000**

Hold the reserve until the initial valid September review. Release requires normal approval and one of these conditions:

1. Azerion reports CPA at or below $400 after at least 30 applications and confirms capacity.
2. Search reports Step-5 CPA at or below $450, rank improvements are active, and lost-to-budget exceeds 20% for three consecutive days.
3. Quantcast reaches at least 70% viewability, keeps viewable CPM at or below $2.40, holds exclusion-list inventory at or below 10% of spend, and passes routing checks.

The reserve cannot fund PMax, Native, Meta, CTV, or TikTok.
