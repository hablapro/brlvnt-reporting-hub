## Executive summary

June was GCG's biggest media month of 2026: $117,024 across five lines, up 53% from May, with the Native pilot launching as the fifth channel. The quarter closed at $251,621 (Q3, per the budget tracker).

The June story is scale meeting its limits in different places per channel. Search spend rose 48% but submitted applications fell 12% (76 to 67) and CPA rose $200 to $336. The auction data explains it: every campaign loses 64-76% of available impressions to ad rank and only 9-13% to budget, so June's extra dollars bought worse auctions rather than more volume. The efficiency lever for July is ad rank (bids, quality, ad strength), not budget.

Paid social scaled 151% and its delivery stayed healthy: 71% of link clicks became landing-page views and site analytics capture roughly 57% of clicks as sessions, so the measurement chain works. The channel still runs on a traffic objective; the planned shift to a conversion objective is now the top paid-social action.

Programmatic reach scaled on purpose: 37.2M impressions at a $0.82 CPM reaching 23.2M devices. The quality bill came with it. Viewability fell to 46.9%, well under the 70% standard, and we delivered an 18-site blocklist covering 32% of June spend plus a viewability-floor recommendation.

The client's own funnel shows June submitted applications up 7% month over month (300 to 322) with approval rates steady at 45%. The quarter ran 14% below Q1 on applications while media scaled, and the start-to-submit step is where volume dilutes. Fixing funnel conversion is worth more than the next media dollar.

## Site traffic and funnel — the June read

Spanish-audience traffic on the US property held steady near 70K sessions from January through May and rose to 80,231 in June (+15%), with unique visitors up 40% to 41,795. There is no organic decline underneath GCG; the traffic base is stable and June's lift is paid.

The client funnel tells the sharper story. June submitted applications rose 7% to 322 while approval (45%) and funded (28%) rates held. But Q2 closed 14% below Q1 on applications (1,033 vs 1,203) while working media scaled 5x quarter over quarter, and the application-start rate diluted from 55% in January to 4% in June as traffic grew. Volume is arriving; conversion is the constraint. The start-to-submit step and the funded step are where the next accounts are, and both are funnel work, not media work.
